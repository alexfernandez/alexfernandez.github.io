# El tinnitus y los acúfenos

## Tratamientos

Según [Baguley _et al_](https://www.thelancet.com/action/showPdf?pii=S0140-6736%2813%2960142-7),
el tinnitus no tiene cura.

En la conclusión dicen (y traduzco):

> Progress of research into tinnitus is clear, including systematic reviews of treatments,
> basic science research on mechanisms, and development of novel approaches to treatment.
> Once otological pathology has been excluded or treated,
> the treatment of choice for tinnitus involves the interweaving of education,
> sound therapy, and counselling, informed by the principles of Cognitive behavioural therapy.
> Although the benefits of such treatment are small, and the perception of tinnitus does not stop,
> it does improve quality of life and reduce awareness of, and reaction to, tinnitus.
